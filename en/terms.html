Effective August 27, 2026 · 한국어

1. What this service does

Preaching Lab takes the sermon video or recording you send us, transcribes it, and writes you a review of its structure, handling of the text, delivery, and application. As sermons accumulate, we show you how those things have changed over time.

2. What the report is, and what it is not

  • The report is a reference to help you prepare. It is not a grade.
  • We do not rule on whether a sermon is theologically right or wrong, and we do not substitute for pastoral judgment. Where traditions differ, we look at how well the text supports what was said — not at which tradition is correct.
  • A machine read your sermon. It will sometimes be wrong. Every observation is tied to a quote from your own transcript so you can check it.
  • The final judgment is always the preacher's.

We do not publish them, quote them in marketing, or show them to other users. We do not use your sermons to train AI models, and the vendors listed in our Privacy Policy handle them under terms that prohibit the same.

4. What you may send

Send only sermons you preached yourself, or ones whose preacher has given you permission. You may not send:

  • someone else's sermon or video that you have no right to

One thing worth pausing on. Sermons often name real people — someone in the hospital, a family in trouble, a request made in confidence. When you send us a recording, that goes with it. Nothing in this service shows your report to your congregation or your board, and Section 3 keeps it that way. But whether it was yours to say from the pulpit in the first place remains your call, not ours.
